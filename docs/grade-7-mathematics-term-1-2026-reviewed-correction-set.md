### PDF pages 2–3 — Week 1 Lessons 3–5: Total Value

The three entries move from identifying total value, to explaining its relationship with place value, but Week 1 Lessons 4 and 5 repeat substantially the same outcome and activity.

Suggested progression for teacher review:

- **Lesson 3:** Identify the total value of each digit in given whole numbers using place-value apparatus.
- **Lesson 4:** Explain and demonstrate the relationship between a digit's place value and total value; distinguish the position of a digit from the amount it represents.
- **Lesson 5:** Apply total value to numbers up to hundreds of millions in real-life contexts and solve short application problems.

**Teacher decision required:** Confirm that the progression is appropriate and that Lesson 5 is intended as application/reinforcement rather than a duplicate. Do not change the approved SLOs without authorization.
