## 2. Targeted content alignment findings

The KICD Whole Numbers design (detailed design, printed pages 1–2) groups learning outcomes around place/total value, reading and writing numbers, rounding, classifying even/odd/prime numbers, whole-number operations, and number sequences. Its suggested experiences include using place-value charts for rounding, sorting number cards for even/odd/prime classification, and practising combined operations in correct order.

| Source location | Scheme entry | Finding against KICD | Required disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| PDF page 5 | Rounding off numbers | The stated learning outcome is rounding, but the listed learning experience is reading/writing numbers in words and writing dummy cheques. ## 3. Other sub-strands

- **Factors:** the four divisibility lessons cover divisibility by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11; composite-number factorisation, GCD and LCM are also represented. These align with the KICD Factors outcomes and suggested experiences.
- **Fractions:** comparison, addition, subtraction, multiplication, reciprocals, division, whole-number division by a fraction and fraction sequences are represented. The listed learning experiences generally match KICD's use of cards, cut-outs, models and sequence games.
- **Decimals:** place value, total value, multiplication and division by whole numbers and decimals are represented. The activities generally match KICD's decimal apparatus, models, calculators and IT suggestions.
- **Squares and Square Roots:** squares and square roots of whole numbers, fractions and decimals are represented, with grids/charts, multiplication, factors/division methods and calculators. These generally match KICD.
- **Algebraic Expressions:** forming expressions from real-life/classified objects and simplifying expressions through addition, subtraction, multiplication and division are represented. The listed activities generally align with KICD.
- **Linear Equations:** forming and solving linear equations in one unknown, role play and real-life applications are represented. The listed activities generally align with KICD.
