# ECG Architecture: Canonical CBE Content Architecture (Phase 2)

## 1. Overview
Phase 2 establishes the canonical Competency-Based Education (CBE) curriculum domain model for the ECG platform. The central architectural principle is:

> **Curriculum knowledge belongs to ECG. Teaching implementation belongs to the school.**

This ensures a strict separation between:
1. **Curriculum Domain**: What must be taught according to Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD) standards.
2. **School Domain**: How, when, and under what configuration a specific school teaches it (from Phase 1 `SchoolProfile` & `TeachingConfig`).
3. **Generation Domain**: Pedagogical artifacts generated by ECG/Gemini (Schemes of Work, Lesson Plans, Assessments) referencing canonical curriculum IDs.

---

## 2. Canonical Curriculum Hierarchy
```text
Curriculum (e.g. KICD CBC v2026)
  └── GradeLevel (e.g. Grade 7, Grade 8)
       └── LearningArea (e.g. Mathematics, English)
            └── Strand (e.g. Numbers)
                 └── SubStrand (e.g. Whole Numbers up to Millions)
                      └── LearningOutcome (Specific Learning Outcomes)
```

## 4. Key Architectural Rules
1. **No School Assumptions in Curriculum**: Canonical curriculum entities must never contain `schoolId`, school names, term durations, or LWPS-specific parameters.
2. **Curriculum Versioning**: Curricula are versioned (`curriculumId` and `version`), allowing historical generated artifacts to remain traceable even when curriculum updates occur.
3. **Deterministic Validation**: Referential integrity is enforced programmatically (`CanonicalCurriculumService.validateCurriculumReference`) rather than relying on LLMs.
4. **Gemini as Enhancer, Not Source of Truth**: Gemini receives structured curriculum context and outputs pedagogical artifacts referencing canonical curriculum IDs.
